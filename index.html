How to use Gizmo

1 · Load a pack

Drop a ZIP containing an FBX and its textures anywhere on the page — or a bare .fbx, or loose files. You can add more files later (e.g. the FBX first, textures after); everything re-wires on each drop. TGA works; TIFF/EXR/PSD can't be decoded by browsers and will be listed as unused.

2 · How auto-wiring works

Texture paths inside the FBX are remapped to your files by filename. FBX has no roughness or metallic, so those are matched by name suffix and grouped by shared prefix (e.g. Robot_Head_*) so multi-material packs land correctly:

_BaseColor · _Albedo · _Diffuse · _D _Normal · _NormalGL/DX · _N _Roughness · _Rough · _R _Metallic · _Metalness · _M _AO · _AmbientOcclusion · _Occlusion _Emissive · _Emission · _Glow _ORM / _ARM packed → R=AO · G=Rough · B=Metal _Height · _Displacement · _Bump _Opacity · _Alpha · _Mask

Resolution tails (_2K, _4096), version tags and UDIM numbers are ignored when matching.

3 · Read the report

from FBX the FBX itself referenced this file  ·  name match wired by suffix convention  ·  packed one channel of an ORM/ARM map  ·  assumed best guess — double-check it.

Dashed rows are empty slots. Below the materials you'll find files that went unused (with the reason) and anything the FBX references that you didn't provide. A green line means the pack is complete.

4 · Viewer

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, two-finger drag (or right-drag) to pan. Toolbar: lighting presets, wireframe, shadows, re-frame, and PNG capture of the current view. On a wide screen, drag the report's left edge to resize it.

5 · The model bar

Every FBX you load gets a tile in the strip along the bottom of the viewport — scroll it sideways if the pack has more than fits. Previews are rendered in the background, one model at a time, so tiles start as spinners and fill in; the triangle count sits in the corner of each. Click a tile to show that model — the report follows it. Models above the strip hides and shows it.
